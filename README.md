# Offshore Hybrid Platform

## Executive summary
A modular offshore complex combining high-density GPU compute, macroalgae/sargassum processing (anaerobic digestion and hydrothermal liquefaction), and marine-grade BESS utility storage. This repository is a concept-stage, auditable engineering and cost basis—not a final design or safety case.

## Architecture
Each 50 m x 50 m jacket cell supports 4–5 deck tiers: cellar deck +20–28 m; middle tiers +28–46 m; weather deck +46–60 m. Bridge-linked cells form resilient clusters with separated fire, electrical, process and personnel systems. A 10-hectare (100,000 m²) mega-complex can be assembled across an 80–100 km² ocean concession, subject to metocean, lease, cable, environmental and marine-traffic constraints.

Subsystems include GPU halls and liquid cooling; nitrogen-purged shells, UPS and MV distribution; intake/dewatering/digestion/HTL/biogas treatment; containerized BESS, switchgear and energy management; utilities, accommodation, access, export and emergency systems.

## Design basis and economics
Use site-specific hindcast, bathymetry, geotechnical investigation and certified load cases before design. Concept ranges include $80–250m/cell substructure, $85–310m topsides, $200–500m per 500 MWh BESS package, $160–770m compute infrastructure, and $170–800m biorefinery. Aggregated 10-ha scenarios are low $2–4B, central $4–8B, high $8–15B+; ranges are screening estimates, not quotations.
