# Metocean and Gulf of Guinea environmental basis

This is a comparative screening basis, not a design environmental database. Obtain directional hindcasts, buoy/satellite calibration, extremes and joint distributions for the selected coordinates.

## Comparative screening
Gulf of Guinea: generally lower-to-moderate locally generated seas (concept average Hs 0.5–2.5 m), strong seasonal convection and current effects; long-period swell can govern air gap and fatigue. North Sea: frequent energetic wind seas, severe winter storms and mature offshore design practice. Gulf of Mexico: hurricanes, storm surge, waves and current; rapid intensification and directional combinations matter. Southeast Asia: monsoon seasons, typhoons in some basins, currents and complex shallow-water transformation.

## Key phenomena
Use Hs, Tp/Tz, direction, wind, current, water level and spectral shape, with extremes and persistence. South Atlantic/Southern Ocean swell can produce Tp=14–22 s; long-period energy increases global load, heave/pitch response and resonance risk even when Hs is modest. Convective line squalls may produce 40–60+ kt gusts and abrupt wind shifts. Guinea Current screening range is 1–2 kt, requiring current drag, scour and installation analysis.

Ghana shelf bathymetry screening spans approximately 20 m nearshore to 125 m toward the shelf break; confirm with multibeam survey, geophysical hazards and soil borings. Depth drives jacket geometry, pile length, installation vessel selection and cable routing.

## Design workflow
Define hindcast period and quality; calibrate against observations; derive 1-, 10-, 50-, 100-year and operational contours; combine tide, surge, wave crest, current and wind directionally; apply shoaling/refraction/breaking and local bathymetry; check air gap, green water, fatigue, scour, station keeping and weather windows. Document climate-change allowances, uncertainty and return-period convention. Cross-reference [structural engineering](structural-engineering.md) and [sources](sources-and-citations.md).
